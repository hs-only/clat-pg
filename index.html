Please read the instructions carefully

A. General Instructions & Exam Pattern:

  1. Test Title:
  2. Total duration of this examination is 120 minutes, containing 120 Multiple Choice Questions across 20 Comprehension Passages.
  3. Marking Scheme:
    • Each correct answer carries +1.00 (One) mark.
    • Each incorrect answer attracts a negative marking of -0.25 (Zero point two five) marks.
    • No marks (0.00) are deducted for unattempted questions.
  4. Save & Resume Later: Your answers, question palette states, and remaining timer are auto-saved after every action. You can click "💾 Save & Resume Later" at any time to pause the test and continue later from the exact same question and timer.
  5. The Question Palette displayed on the right side of screen will show the status of each question using one of the following five official TCS iON symbols:
1 You have NOT visited the question yet.
2 You have NOT answered the question.
3 You have answered the question.
4 You have NOT answered the question, but have marked the question for review.
5 The question(s) "Answered and Marked for Review" WILL BE CONSIDERED FOR EVALUATION.

B. Navigating & Answering a Question:

  1. Click on the question number in the Question Palette at the right of your screen to go to that numbered question directly.
  2. Click on Save & Next to save your answer for the current question and then go to the next question.
  3. Click on Mark for Review & Next to save your answer for the current question (if an option is selected), mark it for review, and then go to the next question.
  4. To deselect your chosen answer, click on the Clear Response button at the bottom bar.
